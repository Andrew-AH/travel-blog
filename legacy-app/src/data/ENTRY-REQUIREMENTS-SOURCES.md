# Entry requirements sources

Reviewed: 2026-09-24.

The travel tips selector links visitors to official government resources. It does not calculate visa eligibility or determine which charges a traveller must pay. Visitors need to check their passport, residence, purpose and length of stay, route and travel dates with the relevant authority.

Keep changing eligibility rules, exemptions, prices, application deadlines and service launch dates on the official websites. Recheck these links when editing this feature. A resource appearing in the selector does not mean every visitor must apply or pay. These links are not a guarantee that every visa, tourist tax, local fee or arrival requirement is covered, or that entry will be granted.

## Worldwide checks

- [IATA Travel Centre](https://www.iata.org/en/travel-centre/): official IATA landing page directing travellers to its worldwide passport, visa and health requirements checker. IATA is an airline industry association, not a government. The underlying iatatravelcentre.com checker returned HTTP 403 to the research crawler, so the feature links to IATA’s verified official landing page.
- [Smartraveller: Getting a foreign visa](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/getting-foreign-visa): Australian Government guidance on checking visa requirements with the destination authority.

## Indonesia and Bali

- [Indonesia visa selection](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/web/visa-selection): Directorate General of Immigration visa selection tool.
- [Love Bali](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/): Bali Provincial Government tourist levy information, including exemptions. The Bali levy is separate from an Indonesian visa.
- [All Indonesia](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/): official arrival declaration service. Travellers should check when to submit information for their journey.

## France, Greece, Italy and Switzerland

- [France-Visas visa checker](https://www.france-visas.gouv.fr/en/web/france-visas/assistant-visa): current official wizard linked from the France-Visas site map. The older `/en/visa-wizard` URL returned an error during research.
- [Greece visa requirements](https://www.mfa.gr/en/services/visas-for-foreigners-traveling-to-greece/countries-requiring-or-not-requiring-a-visa/): Ministry of Foreign Affairs requirements by nationality and passport type.
- [Visa for Italy](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/?lang=en_US): Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation checker using nationality, residence, purpose and stay length. The older `/home/en` URL returned an error during research; this English homepage loaded successfully.
- [Swiss entry requirements](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/themen/einreise/info-einreise.html): State Secretariat for Migration entry and visa guidance by nationality.
- [European Commission Schengen visa guidance](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/visa-policy/applying-schengen-visa_en): supporting guidance on who needs a short-stay visa and which consulate handles an itinerary.
- [Official ETIAS information](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias): shared status resource for these four destinations.

As reviewed on 2026-09-24, the official ETIAS site says operations are planned for the last quarter of 2026 and travellers do not need to take action yet. Do not present ETIAS as currently open for applications or required from every visitor. The UI deliberately uses **Check ETIAS status** without embedding a launch date, fee or universal application instruction.

## Japan

- [Ministry of Foreign Affairs visa guidance](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/): nationality-specific visa information and links to exemptions.
- [Short-stay visa exemptions](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/short/novisa.html): supporting details on passport conditions and nationality-specific exemptions.
- [Visit Japan Web](https://services.digital.go.jp/en/visit-japan-web/): Digital Agency arrival service for immigration and customs information. This is separate from visa eligibility and is not described as a visa or universal mandatory application.
- [Digital Agency service description](https://www.digital.go.jp/en/policies/visit_japan_web): confirms the official service and links to its landing page.

## New Zealand

- [Visa or NZeTA checker](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visit/what-you-need-to-visit-new-zealand/check-if-you-need-a-visa-or-an-nzeta/): Immigration New Zealand guidance based on the traveller’s passport and journey.
- [International Visitor Levy](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/fees-processing-times-and-refunds/paying-the-international-visitor-levy/): who pays the IVL and who is exempt. Where required, it is collected with the relevant visa or NZeTA application; the UI does not send visitors to make an additional standalone payment.

## South Korea

- [Korea Visa Navigator](https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?LANG_TYPE=EN&MENU_ID=10101): Ministry of Justice checker using nationality, purpose and stay length.
- [K-ETA eligible countries and applicants](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/guide/viewetaalification.do): current applicant criteria and access to exemption guidance.
- [K-ETA temporary exemption extension](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=299707&locale=EN): official notice extending exemptions for designated countries and regions through 2026-12-31 (KST), as reviewed on 2026-09-24.
- [K-ETA FAQ](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboardlist.do?tmpltNm=faq2): further exemptions, including age and traveller-status conditions.

K-ETA eligibility to apply is not the same as a requirement to apply. The temporary exemption does not cover every passport, and other exemptions also exist. The UI directs travellers to check current requirements without embedding the temporary exemption deadline or deciding eligibility.

## United Kingdom

- [Check if you need a UK visa](https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa): official questionnaire for the traveller’s nationality and plans.
- [UK electronic travel authorisation](https://www.gov.uk/eta): Home Office guidance on ETA requirements and exemptions. The UI does not imply that every traveller needs both a visa and an ETA.
