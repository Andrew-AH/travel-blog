# Travel safety links

Official URLs checked on 24 September 2026. The Travel Tips country safety tool links to the source; it does not copy, calculate, cache or display advisory levels. The selector lists the eight international countries on the owner's destination map. Australia is a domestic destination for this Australian audience and is not a Smartraveller overseas advisory.

## Australian Government: Smartraveller

The live [destination directory](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations) was opened and its country links followed to verify the pages below.

- [Indonesia, including Bali](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia)
- [France](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/france)
- [Greece](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece)
- [Italy](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/italy)
- [Japan](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/japan)
- [South Korea](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/south-korea-republic-korea)
- [Switzerland](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/switzerland)
- [United Kingdom, including London](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/united-kingdom)

The country pages provide safety, health, local laws, travel/entry requirements and local contacts. The page directs readers to check advice before booking and departure because advice can change. The site does not claim affiliation with Smartraveller.

## Advice from other governments

These official landing pages were opened and verified. They are optional links for readers travelling on another passport, rather than country-specific results from the selector.

- New Zealand: [SafeTravel](https://www.safetravel.govt.nz/)
- United Kingdom: [FCDO foreign travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice)
- Canada: [Travel advice and advisories](https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/advisories)

All outbound advice links open in a new tab with `noopener noreferrer`. The main button communicates the selected country and new-tab behavior in its accessible name. Other links include an equivalent screen-reader notice. The selector has a visible label and a live explanatory note; choosing a country never navigates automatically.
