# Australian destination photography

Prepared 24 September 2026. These are licensed illustrative photographs of the named destinations, not the blog owner's personal photographs. All four source pages explicitly identify their photographs as free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). No image-generation tool was used.

Downloaded JPEG originals are retained as `{destination}-original.jpg` in `public/images/destinations/`. Served WebP derivatives are center-cropped to 800 × 600 and 400 × 300 at Sharp quality 77. No synthetic alterations or recoloring were applied. All four 800 × 600 images were visually inspected after processing.
