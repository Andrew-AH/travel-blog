# Destination photo sources

These four photographs were sourced for the initial atlas. The Bali, Japan, and Sydney photographs remain in the owner's confirmed collection; the Lisbon photograph is retained but no longer displayed. These are licensed third-party photographs, not the owner's personal photographs. No image-generation tool was used for these assets.

All four source pages identify the work as free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Source pages and downloads were checked on 24 September 2026. The downloaded 1600 px JPEG versions are retained beside the served WebP derivatives in `public/images/destinations/`. The WebP files are center-cropped to 800 × 600 and 400 × 300 pixels and compressed at quality 77 using the repository's Sharp dependency; no synthetic alterations or recoloring were applied. Regenerate them using `node scripts/optimize-destination-images.mjs`.

The `polignano` and `koh-tao` WebP variants are resized copies of the project's generated coastal and hammock illustrations. They are labeled as illustrative in their postcard views; the original PNGs and prompts remain documented in the main asset credits and design notes.
