# Malaysia and Singapore destination photography

These licensed third-party photographs illustrate destinations named by the blog owner. They are not presented as the owner's personal photographs or as evidence of specific attractions visited. The source pages and [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) were checked on 24 September 2026. Both source pages identify their photographs as free to use under that license.

Downloaded JPEG source images are retained in `public/images/destinations/`. Each served image is a centered 800 × 600 or 400 × 300 WebP crop, produced with Sharp at quality 77. No image generation, synthetic changes, or recoloring were used.
